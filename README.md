# Inkit — Support / 使用帮助

Inkit is a drawing and sculpting studio for iPad. Draw strokes, explore organic or relief geometry, sculpt the surface, and finish with materials and lighting.

Inkit 是一款 iPad 绘画与雕刻工具：从线条生成立体形态，雕刻表面，再用材质和灯光完成作品。

需要帮助或反馈问题，请邮件联系 **daftlamb@gmail.com**，说明 iPad 型号、系统版本、Inkit 版本和复现步骤。请勿发送密码或无关的私人文件。

## Quick start / 快速开始

1. Draw on the drawing canvas. Your model is generated in the model viewport. Apple Pencil and touch input are supported.
2. Switch between organic and relief geometry; explore symmetry, shape and material controls.
3. Open the 3D workspace to Raise, Dent, Smooth, Move, Pinch or Flatten the surface.
4. Open Render to adjust materials, lighting, background and effects. Detailed models can take longer to process.
5. Export PNG images or GLB/STL models through the system share sheet. Export `.inkit` projects to keep an editable backup.

在绘画区画下线条，在模型区查看立体结果。通过对称、形态和材质面板调整作品；进入 3D 模式雕刻，进入 Render 调整灯光和效果。导出菜单支持 PNG、GLB、STL 与可再次打开的 `.inkit` 项目。

## Projects and troubleshooting / 项目与故障排查

Projects are stored locally. Export important projects regularly. Removing the app may delete its local data. For complex projects, wait for model generation to finish before exporting. STL exports have not been certified for watertightness or printability; check them in your intended downstream software.

项目保存在设备本地，建议定期导出备份。删除应用可能删除本地数据。复杂模型请等待生成完成后导出。STL 尚未做打印闭合性认证，请在后续软件中检查。
