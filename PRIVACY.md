# Inkit Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective date: October 22, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

Inkit is developed by Tao Zhang. Contact: **daftlamb@gmail.com**.

## Data collection

Inkit does not require an account and does not send your drawings or projects to a developer-operated server. The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. Its drawing engine, fonts and rendering assets are bundled with the app for offline use.

## Local projects and diagnostics

Drawings, settings, editable projects and engine recovery diagnostics are stored on your device. The app uses an on-device loopback HTTP server to serve its bundled rendering files to its web view. This server is local to your device and does not upload projects. Local data may be included in device backups managed by iPadOS and your Apple account settings.

## Files you choose to share

When you import or export a file, the system file picker and share sheet handle the destinations you select. Files you share with third-party services are subject to those services’ policies. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request.

## Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases and may provide app diagnostics according to your device and Apple account settings. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple’s handling of such information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

## Control and deletion

You can remove exported files in Files and delete the app to remove its local app data. Manage device backups through iPadOS settings. To request deletion of a support email you sent, contact **daftlamb@gmail.com**.

## Changes

This page will be updated if the app’s privacy practices change.
